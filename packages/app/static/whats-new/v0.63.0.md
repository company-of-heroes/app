This release is about sharing your games: upload and publish replays with a full compose flow, and sign in on the website with Steam.

## Upload and share your replays

Drop a `.rec` file and we parse the match for you — map, duration, players, chat, and timeline. Add a title and a markdown description, link Steam IDs when the replay is missing them, then hit **Publish**.

Your replay shows up under community replays so others can watch, vote, and comment. You can also publish a match you already own from the companion.

![Upload replay screen with map preview, title, description, and Steam-linked players](/whats-new/images/v0.63.0/share-replays.png)

## Sign in with Steam on the website

On [coh1stats.com](https://coh1stats.com) you can now log in with Steam. We find or create your account from your Steam ID — same community profile as the desktop app, no separate password to remember.

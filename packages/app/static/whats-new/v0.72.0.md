This release is about earning your place: service ribbons for your rewards, tournaments you can run yourself, maps in the wiki, and keybindings that send whole key sequences.

## Earn your ribbons

Rewards now show as a ribbon rack on your profile and dashboard, with 200 service ribbons: 25 per faction, 25 global ones, and ranked series that add stars or pips as you level up. Each ranked series shows one ribbon, for the highest level you reached.

On your own profile and dashboard you also see what is next: the 3 rewards you are closest to show faded with a lock, and the tooltip tells you how far along you are. See every reward and what it takes to earn it on the [rewards page](https://coh1stats.com/wiki/rewards) in the wiki.

A new reward tracks the highest Relic ladder rank (up to 20) you reached in automatch, 1v1 to 4v4, per faction or mode. Rewards are now earned per Steam account: wins, streaks, rating and hours played count only for the Steam account that played them, while comments, reputation and streaming count for every Steam account you linked. The Streamer badge works the same way and goes to the Steam account you stream on.

![Player profile with a ribbon rack of earned rewards and three locked rewards under Next](/whats-new/images/v0.72.0/earn-ribbons.png)

## Tournaments

Join a tournament on [coh1stats.com](https://coh1stats.com/tournaments), play your matches with **Start tournament game** on the dashboard and results come in automatically. Visitors without an account can sign in with Steam right from the join button.

Tournament hosts get more control:

- Add players by Steam ID, also right in the create form, and remove them until the start.
- Drag players to change the seeding, or generate the bracket early and start with it later. Only staff and hosts see it until then.
- Swap players in a running bracket by dragging them onto each other (unplayed matches only).
- Give the quarterfinals and semifinals their own match length, like the final.
- Save a tournament as a draft, and delete or restore it.

## Maps in the wiki

The [wiki](https://coh1stats.com/wiki/maps) now has every built-in map, classic and Operations, on its loading-screen image with victory, fuel, munition and strategic points, territory sectors and starting positions. Built-in maps in a tournament map pool link straight to their page.

The wiki also shows who builds what, building and research prerequisites, what a doctrine pick unlocks, medal reward units, and much more detail on weapons: range bands, timings, rear penetration and area damage. Lots of unit stats, prices and crew counts are corrected too.

## Key sequences

A keybinding can now send a whole sequence of keys to the game, with repeats. Press **F** once and the game receives `F ×4 → Tab → F ×4 → …` to reinforce every squad in your selection.

![Keybinding that turns a single F press into F x4, Tab, repeated four times](/whats-new/images/v0.72.0/keybindings-sequence.png)

## Also new

- Copy button next to every Steam ID.
- Player search looks in stored players first, sorts by relevance and loads more while you scroll.
- Faster data loading across the app.
- Replay comments have their own Comments tab with the comment count.
- Player profiles and the dashboard use a new header with a stat strip, and no longer jump while loading.
- Delete, remove and other destructive buttons are red, with a red confirm button in the in-app dialog.
- Text-to-speech no longer shows ElevenLabs errors or turns itself off while StreamElements is selected.

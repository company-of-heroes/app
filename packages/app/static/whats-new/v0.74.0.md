This release is about the community: become a supporter, leaderboards that show who is moving, a replays page that helps you find the games worth watching, and guides written by players.

## Become a supporter

You can now support coh1stats with a monthly subscription of €4.99, paid through Mollie with iDEAL, card or PayPal. In return you get a **supporter medal** next to your name across the app and the website, **supporter ribbons** that unlock as your supporter months add up, a shareable **player card** with your best ELO and rank per army, as a page and as a PNG, and your name on the [supporters page](https://coh1stats.com/supporters).

Hit **Become a supporter** in the header to start. You can cancel or resume at any time from your account; your medal stays until the paid month ends, and the ribbons you earned stay with you.

## Leaderboards that move

The leaderboards are redesigned around what changed. Every row shows the rank movement since yesterday and since last week, and the side panel lists the **climbers**, **fallers** and **new** entries, the **hot streaks** and which countries fill the top 200.

Your own position is always there, also when you are outside the top 200, with **Jump to me** to scroll straight to your row. Sort the ladder by rank, win rate, games or streak, and filter by country or by a minimum number of games.

## Discover replays

The [replays page](https://coh1stats.com/replays) opens on a new **Discover** view: featured replays, pro 1v1s, big battles, fast games and the most discussed replays in their own rows, followed by the latest shared replays. Any replay plays with one click in the game, and the app suggests which of your own recent matches are worth sharing.

The home page is calmer too: one row of recent matches, and live lobbies shown as cards in the same grid as the replays.

## Guides

Any signed-in user can write a [guide](https://coh1stats.com/guides) and send it for review; staff and editors publish it. Guides are filed under categories, support cover and inline images, wiki links and translations, and later edits wait for review too. Admins hand out the new **editor** role from the users page. The wiki also gained pages for every upgrade.

## Claim your player account

Found yourself on a leaderboard or a player page without an account? Hit **Claim** and sign in with Steam to link that player to you, right from the website.

## Labels and ribbons

Labels now work like rewards: a label can have unlock conditions and is given automatically to every Steam ID you linked that meets them. The **Veteran** label reaches every ranked 1v1 and 2v2 player who ever held rank 20 on two ladders.

Ribbons show their faction with wide national edge stripes, all-faction rewards get their own ribbon, and supporter rewards have their own category on the [rewards page](https://coh1stats.com/wiki/rewards).

## Also new

- Tournament pages show the host with avatar and profile link, and staff and hosts can upload their own champion medals (PNG or SVG).
- Linux download buttons let you pick the AppImage or the .deb package.
- Recent form on the dashboard and player profiles shows the last 10 results as compact blocks across the full width.
- Match history shows player country flags as plain flags, without the round crop and ring.
- The app sidebar and website header show the title only; the logo is gone.
- The background of a selected tab or toggle option grows in from the centre.
- Lighter faction icons in replay and live lobby cards.
- Wiki pages no longer clip the header and keep the side column readable in narrow windows.
- Tournament player lists show the country flag of every player.
- ELO history on the performance tab loads again when you open it.
- Reward unlock notifications are no longer lost, matches recorded twice count once, unfinished matches break win streaks, and secret rewards stay hidden until you earn them.
- A label staff remove from a player stays removed.
- The creator is now Red™ 🏅, with Twitch links pointing to twitch.tv/commanderredttv.

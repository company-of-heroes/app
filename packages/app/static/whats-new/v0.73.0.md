This release is about rivals and reach: a head to head record against every opponent, comparing yourself with any player, and the app and Replay Manager on Linux.

## Head to head

See how you do against the players in your lobby. Every opponent you played before gets a chip with your record against them, like **3–1**. Click it for the breakdown by mode and matchup. The live match now opens on its own history page, with the head to head right there.

Player profiles show your record against that player in the header. Open it for a breakdown by mode and by matchup, and expand any line to see the games behind it. The record counts every Steam account you linked, not only the one you are playing on.

## Compare yourself with any player

Hit **Compare** on a player profile, or **Full comparison** from a head to head, to put yourself side by side with that player: ELO, win rate, recent form, factions, maps and your games against each other.

Linked more than one Steam account? Pick which of your accounts to count, and the comparison updates right away.

## Linux

The companion app and the Replay Manager now run on Linux, for Company of Heroes through Steam Proton. Download the AppImage or the deb package from [coh1stats.com](https://coh1stats.com), and updates arrive automatically like on Windows.

Game detection, keybindings, chat and fair play work through X11. On a Wayland desktop that needs XWayland, which most distributions run by default; without it, the app shows a notice in Settings.

> Fair play on Linux also checks the native libraries loaded into the game. The [privacy policy](https://coh1stats.com/privacy) describes what is reported.

## Also new

- Signing up with email and password sends a verification email again.
- Going back from a match or replay keeps the history page you came from.
- Reward tooltips show the ribbon larger and in colour.
- The coh1stats.com home page shows running tournaments and tournaments open for sign-up at the very top.
- Map statistics on the website and the wiki rewards page load much faster.

This release is about knowing the game: community statistics on coh1stats.com, a wiki with the stats of every unit, building, doctrine and weapon, and a new CoH Global Community look.

## A new look: CoH Global Community

The app and the website now carry the new **CoH Global Community** logo, in the sidebar, in the site header, as the favicon and as the app icon on your desktop and taskbar. Same app, same account and the same data, just a new name and badge that ties it to the wider Company of Heroes community.

## Community stats on coh1stats.com

The new [Stats](https://coh1stats.com/stats) page shows how CoH is played right now, built from the matches companion users stored and the replays you upload.

- **Maps**: how often each map is played, how long games last and whether Allies or Axis win more. Click a map to filter all stats to it.
- **Factions and matchups**: win and pick rates per faction, and how each Allied faction does against Wehrmacht and Panzer Elite.
- **Doctrines, units, openings and upgrades**: which doctrines get picked and win, what gets built most and how players open, all from analysed replays.
- **Did you know?**: the most active player, the longest match, the busiest hour and more.

Switch between 1v1 to 4v4, Basic Match and Skirmish, and pick all time, the last year, 90, 30 or 7 days, or your own date range. The homepage shows a summary too.

## The wiki

The new [Wiki](https://coh1stats.com/wiki) lists every unit, building, doctrine and weapon per faction, with its stats from the game files: cost, build time, health, veterancy bonuses, weapons with damage, range and accuracy, and what each upgrade or doctrine unlock gives. Hover an entry for a quick look or open its page for the full details, and search to jump straight to a unit. Staff add tips on when to use what.

![Wehrmacht wiki page with buildings and units per tier, and a popover showing the Sdkfz 251 Halftrack's cost, health and veterancy](/whats-new/images/v0.70.0/wiki.png)

## Game stats in your replays

The same data shows up in the app: hover an icon on the replay timeline or in the statistics to see a unit's health, weapons and veterancy, what an upgrade adds (bonus health, accuracy, …), the weapon a package gives you and what a doctrine unlock does.

## Also new

- VAC and game bans show prominently on player profiles.
- Profile links show the full URL in a popover before opening.
- Replay chat marks your own messages with a dot, all-chat with an [all] prefix, and shows the original under translated messages.
- Skirmishes against the AI are detected from the replay and labelled Skirmish instead of Basic Match.
- British Field Support and Armor Command Trucks show as themselves in replays instead of US Airborne.
- In replays where two players share a name, each keeps their own doctrine and actions.
- Repeated clicks on the same ability show once on the replay timeline.

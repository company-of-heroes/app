This release is about trust: a signed Windows installer, a new fair-play screenshot path we are carefully rolling out, and rating players on their profile.

## Signed Windows builds 🎉

Installers are finally code-signed. Windows can verify who published the app, so you should see fewer SmartScreen warnings when you install or update.

We only collect the game and fair-play data the companion needs. More detail is in our [privacy policy](https://coh1stats.com/privacy). The [source code](https://github.com/fknoobs/app) is open — you can always review it yourself.

## Fair-play screenshots under test

We are testing a new way to capture fair-play screenshots — using the game’s own print-screen path first, with window capture as a fallback.

> We are watching screenshot quality and fair play closely. If something looks off, tell us — your reports help us tune this.

## Rate players on their profile

You can upvote or downvote players on their profile. The score sits next to their name so good teammates (and the opposite) stand out across the community.

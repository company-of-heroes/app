This release is about reading a replay like the game does: an in-game style build timeline with costs and descriptions, a new Replay Manager for your playback folder, and a cleaner look across the app.

## See every order on a timeline

Open a replay or a match and pick a player: their whole game is laid out on a timeline, just like in-game. Structures, units, defenses, upgrades, abilities and doctrine choices each get their own row, with the real action icons on faction and doctrine art. Cancelled production and upgrades show up too, and Wehrmacht units carry their veterancy stripes.

Hover an icon to see what it cost and the in-game description, or click it to pin the popover. **Ctrl + scroll** to zoom and drag to pan. You also get an estimate of how many resources each player spent.

![Replay timeline with structures and units on rows, and a popover showing the Sniper's cost and description](/whats-new/images/v0.68.0/timeline.png)

## Meet the Replay Manager

The **Company of Heroes - Replay Manager** is a small, separate desktop app for the replays in your local playback folder. Browse, search, rename and delete them without opening the game. It works fully offline: no account, and nothing gets uploaded. Grab it from the banner on [coh1stats.com](https://coh1stats.com), which also explains how it differs from this companion app.

## Also new

- Faction flags use the same round icon everywhere: stats, match history, leaderboards, performance and the dashboard.
- Bigger tooltips that match the popover style.
- Modals open and close instantly.
- Toggle groups show each option as a button, with the selected one in a lighter colour.
- Slimmer scrollbars with rounded thumbs.
- On coh1stats.com, the site no longer scrolls sideways on mobile and replay chat is easier to read on small screens.

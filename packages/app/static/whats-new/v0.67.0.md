This release is about getting your matches right: switching between your Steam accounts on the dashboard, correct ELO and ranked stats right after a game, and no more duplicate or mixed-up matches.

## Switch between your Steam accounts

If you've linked more than one Steam account, the dashboard profile has a new **Switch account** button next to **Update profile**. Pick an account to see its ratings, recent matches and today's games. The account you're playing on is marked **In game**, and when the game logs in again the dashboard jumps back to it.

![Switch account dropdown on the dashboard listing linked Steam accounts, with the in-game account marked](/whats-new/images/v0.67.0/account-switcher.png)

## Better match results

- Right after a game, the match page shows ELO, wins and losses, not just names and CPM.
- Basic matches are no longer shown, titled or filtered as ranked, and the ELO chart opens on ranked.
- Faction and map stats only count ranked games of the profile you're looking at, not your other linked Steam accounts.
- A replay or your next game no longer gets attached to the previous match when the game log skips the end of a match.
- Duplicate copies of the same match are hidden from your history.
- Live lobby results update again.

## Also new on coh1stats.com

- The best replays get a spotlight, with upload prompts so you can share yours.
- Replays published from a match show player rank and level, both in the replay list and on the replay page.

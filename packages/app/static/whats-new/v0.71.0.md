This release is about competing: tournaments from sign-up to the final, automatic results and notifications, and a cleaner, more consistent look across the app and coh1stats.com.

## Tournaments

Open **Tournaments** in the sidebar or on [coh1stats.com](https://coh1stats.com/tournaments) to find upcoming and running tournaments. Each one has its own banner, logo, rules and map pool (custom maps included), and runs as single elimination, double elimination or round robin. Players are seeded by ELO.

- **Sign up** in a click and accept the rules. Registration closes automatically, and if the rules change you're asked to accept them again.
- **Play your match** with **Start tournament game** on the dashboard. Propose a time to your opponent and get a reminder, find their Steam profile in one click, and keep an eye on the deadline for each round.
- **Results come in automatically** from the game, with a celebration popup when you win. Something went wrong? Use **Report a problem** and staff will sort it out and leave you a note.
- **Stay in the loop**: the Updates tab has staff posts and automatic updates for rule changes, deadlines, the start and the finish. You get a notification in the app and on Windows, and personal notices when your next match is ready, when you're knocked out and where you finished. On the website a notification bell keeps track.
- **New to tournaments?** A step-by-step guide walks you through it.

Tournament games stay hidden until the tournament ends, so nobody can scout your build. Once it's over you get the replays tab with every game, statistics, a hall of fame, and the winner gets a champion medal on their player profile. Watch along with the stream link, featured match and LIVE markers.

Running and open tournaments show up on your dashboard and on the website home page.

## A cleaner look

- Inputs, selects, buttons and toggles all share the same height and style. Page tabs are flush, with a gold underline on the active one.
- Every badge uses one style: a solid grey badge with a coloured status dot, readable on images too.
- Every hover hint now uses the styled tooltip instead of the plain browser one.
- Comments are redesigned: compact rows with inline voting and actions, and a comment box with quick replies.

## Also new

- "My replays" is now called **Replay Manager** and "Member replays" is now **Shared Replays**.
- Spotted wrong info on a wiki page? Report it and staff will review it.
- Wiki doctrine trees follow the real unlock order and no longer list units or abilities a tier only changes or replaces.
- The OppBot overlay no longer disappears for a few seconds when the server responds slowly mid-match.

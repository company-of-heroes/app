Going live? The companion now has your back on YouTube too, and every hour you stream counts toward a shiny new Streamer badge.

## Twitch is now Streaming — with YouTube Live

The Twitch page got a new name: **Streaming**. Next to Twitch you'll find a **YouTube** tab. Hit **Connect YouTube**, sign in through coh1stats.com, flip on **Enable YouTube integration**, and your YouTube Live chat works just like Twitch: TTS reads your chat, and the bot posts player stats and messages straight into it.

Connected channels now show up under your name on your profile. Disconnect and they're gone. That's why the Twitch and YouTube URL fields left **Update profile**: your real, connected channel is the link now.

## Earn the Streamer badge

Stream 12 hours of Company of Heroes and you get a purple **Streamer** badge. It sits next to your name in lobbies, leaderboards, comments and replays, and it turns **green** while you're live, so other players know they can tune in. Your progress is on the Streaming page.

![Streaming page with Streamer badge progress and the new YouTube tab](/whats-new/images/v0.66.0/streamer-badge.png)

## Unlock rewards

Rewards are here. Play matches, win streaks, pull off upsets, reach a rating, comment, upload replays... and rewards unlock on their own, with a notification. Past matches count too, so you might already have a few. Unlocked rewards show as icons under your bio, and on your own profile you can see which ones are still locked and how close you are.

## Also new

- Hover a player to see their likes and badges, also inside a match.
- Leaderboard stats tables show total wins, losses and games at the bottom.
- Filters close after **Apply**, and filtered players are highlighted in blue in match lists.
- Player profiles load faster and no longer flicker.
- Safer accounts: a Steam ID can only be linked to one account, match results come straight from Relic, and you stay signed in longer.
